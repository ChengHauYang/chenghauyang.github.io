Cheng-Hau Yang

Postdoctoral Appointee, Argonne National Laboratory

CV (PDF)

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Argonne National Laboratory, currently working with Dr. Mark Messner and previously supervised by Dr. Gary Hu. My work centers on finite-element methods and open-source scientific software for cohesive fracture and multiphysics problems, including the Shifted Cohesive-Zone Method (SCZM), MOOSE–NEML2 constitutive integration, and GPU-accelerated solid mechanics using Kokkos and CUDA.

I received my Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering (Minor in Applied Mathematics) from Iowa State University, advised by Dr. Baskar Ganapathysubramanian and Dr. Adarsh Krishnamurthy, in collaboration with Dr. Guglielmo Scovazzi on the Shifted Boundary Method (SBM) and Finite Cell Method (FCM). During my Ph.D., I also completed a residency at Google X under Dr. Dino Ruić, where I worked on parallel deal.II parasitic extraction solvers and developed a remeshing-free geometry optimization method that reduced iteration runtime by 50%.

In addition to numerical algorithms and HPC software (MOOSE, deal.II, and Dendro), I work on scientific machine learning benchmarks (FlowBench, MPFBench) and neural-network constitutive modeling. I welcome inquiries regarding research collaborations via LinkedIn.
