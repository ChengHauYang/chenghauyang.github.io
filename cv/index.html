Experience

  • 2025–Present

    • MOOSE–NEML2 Integration: Collaborated with Idaho National Laboratory researchers on the v3 migration, package/build infrastructure, and boundary/interface execution (MOOSE #34170, Marlin #198, MOOSE #34422).
    • GPU Computing: Prototyped and benchmarked solid mechanics and NEML2 material updates with Kokkos/CUDA; enabled Jacobian-based automatic scaling for Kokkos kernels (MOOSE #33867).
    • Interface Robustness & Welding: Enabled distributed-mesh splitting and cohesive-interface robustness (MOOSE #32309, #33902, #34283), and improved welding/AM workflows (MOOSE #30300, #30630, #32385; libMesh #4368).
  • 2023
    Ph.D. Resident (CPT) — X (formerly Google X), Google LLC
    • Developed parallel deal.II + MPI parasitic extraction solvers with a Protocol Buffers workflow and GoogleTest suites; built a remeshing-free geometry optimization method that reduced iteration runtime by 50%.
  • 2019–2025
    Graduate Research Assistant — Iowa State University (ComPM Lab & CFSI Lab)
    • SBM & HPC (ComPM Lab): Improved SBM accuracy by 50% via optimal surrogate boundary selection, scaled octree solvers to 1,280 MPI processes and billions of DOFs with k-d tree acceleration, and extended SBM to thermal flows, adaptive mesh refinement, and thin shells.
    • SciML & Multiphysics: Co-created FlowBench (over 10,000 CFD simulations); simulated non-Newtonian two-phase flows with PyTorch constitutive models; built parallel octree FCM and heart-valve FSI models (CFSI Lab).
